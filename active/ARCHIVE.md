This directory holds the **previously active** ProofForge codebase that lived at
the repository root before the V2 cutover.

## Why it is here

On 2026-07-16 the repository root was switched to **ProofForge V2** (formerly
`new_design/`). The prior product tree was moved here as a research / reference
archive only.

## Status

- **Not** the buildable product at repository root.
- **Not** a runtime dependency, oracle, compatibility entry, or fallback for V2.
- Safe to consult for design history, fixtures, and comparative research.
- Do not import `ProofForge.*` from the root V2 tree into this archive or vice versa.
